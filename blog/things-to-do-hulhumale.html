10 Things to Do in Hulhumale', Maldives

HTM Rentals · September 2026 · 6 min read

Hulhumale' is often seen as just a transit stop between the airport and Male' — but guests who stay here quickly discover it has a lot more to offer. As a modern planned city island with beaches, cafes, water sports, and easy access to the best of the Maldives, Hulhumale' deserves more than a passing glance. Here are 10 things to do during your stay.

1 Watch the Sunset at Hulhumale' Beach

The beach stretching along the western side of Hulhumale' offers some of the most beautiful sunset views in the Maldives. The shallow turquoise water, white sand, and lack of resort crowds make it a genuinely special spot. Walk down in the late afternoon, find a quiet patch of sand, and watch the sky turn gold over the Indian Ocean.

2 Dolphin Cruise

An evening dolphin cruise is one of the most popular excursions for guests staying in Hulhumale'. Local tour operators run regular sunset cruises where you can spot spinner dolphins in the open ocean — often in large pods. The experience is magical and very affordable compared to resort-booked excursions.

3 Visit a Sandbank

A short speedboat ride from Hulhumale' will take you to a pristine sandbank in the middle of the ocean — a tiny strip of white sand surrounded by crystal-clear water with nothing else in sight. These day trips typically include snorkeling, lunch, and time to simply relax in complete seclusion.

4 Snorkeling Trip

The waters around Hulhumale' and nearby reefs are rich with marine life. Local tour operators offer guided snorkeling trips to coral reefs where you can see sea turtles, reef sharks, manta rays, and hundreds of species of tropical fish. No experience is needed — guides provide all equipment and instruction.

5 Explore Local Cafes and Restaurants

Hulhumale' has developed a surprisingly good food scene. From local short eats (hedhika) at traditional Maldivian cafes to modern coffee shops and international restaurants, there's plenty to explore on foot. Ask your host for current recommendations — the best spots change regularly as new places open.

6 Day Trip to Male'

The capital city Male' is just 15-20 minutes away by taxi across the Sinamale' Bridge. It's worth spending a half-day exploring the colourful streets, the fish market, the Friday Mosque, and the local markets. Male' gives you an authentic glimpse into everyday Maldivian life that resort islands can't offer.

7 Local Island Hopping

Day trips to nearby local islands like Maafushi, Guraidhoo, or Thulusdhoo are popular from Hulhumale'. These islands have their own beaches, surf breaks, and local culture. Ferry connections make it easy to spend a day exploring and return to your apartment in the evening.

8 Cycling Around Hulhumale'

Hulhumale' has wide, flat roads and a relatively compact layout — making it ideal for cycling. Bicycle rentals are available locally. A morning ride around Phase 1 and Phase 2 lets you discover the island at your own pace, stopping at the beach, local parks, and waterfront areas.

9 Watersports

Jet skiing, kayaking, paddleboarding, and parasailing are all available near Hulhumale' beach. Several operators set up along the beach and offer hourly rentals. It's a fun afternoon activity, especially for families or groups looking for something active.

10 Simply Relax

Sometimes the best thing to do in the Maldives is nothing at all. With a fully equipped apartment at your disposal, you can cook your own meals, watch the ocean from your window, and decompress from the pace of everyday life. Hulhumale' offers the rare combination of island calm with urban convenience — and that's exactly why so many guests extend their stays.
