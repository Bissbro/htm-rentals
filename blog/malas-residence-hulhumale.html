Malas Residence Hulhumale' — What Makes It Special

HTM Rentals · September 2026 · 4 min read

If you're searching for accommodation in Hulhumale' Phase 2, you've likely come across Malas Residence. It's one of the most recognised residential buildings in the area — and for good reason. HTM Rentals operates four fully furnished apartments here, and in this guide we'll explain exactly what makes Malas Residence stand out.

Where is Malas Residence?

Malas Residence is located in Hulhumale' Phase 2, the newer planned extension of Hulhumale' island. The building sits in a quiet part of the neighbourhood while remaining close to the main road, making it easy to access cafes, supermarkets, the beach, and public transport links to Male' and the airport.

The Sinamale' Bridge — connecting Hulhumale' to Male' and the airport — is just a short taxi ride away, making Malas Residence an ideal base for business travellers and tourists alike.

The Building

Malas Residence is a modern multi-storey residential building with secure access, a clean communal environment, and reliable utilities. Unlike older buildings in Male' or early Phase 1 developments, Malas Residence was built to modern standards with proper insulation, reliable water pressure, and stable electricity.

  • Secure building access — controlled entry for residents and guests
  • Modern construction — well-maintained common areas
  • Reliable utilities — stable water, electricity, and internet infrastructure
  • Quiet location — away from the noise of Male' city centre

HTM Rentals Units at Malas Residence

HTM Rentals manages four apartments at Malas Residence — units A-106, A-118, A-207, and A-701. Each unit is fully furnished and equipped with everything you need for a comfortable stay.

  • A-118 — One-bedroom apartment, 32m², cozy and well-equipped

Why Guests Choose Malas Residence

Guests who stay at HTM Rentals in Malas Residence consistently highlight the same things — the peace and quiet, the cleanliness, and the home-like feel that you simply don't get in a hotel room. With a full kitchen, living area, and private bedroom, a week-long stay feels genuinely comfortable rather than transient.

The location also means guests can easily explore Hulhumale' on foot — the beach is walkable, there are several good cafes and restaurants nearby, and getting to Male' for day trips or onward travel is straightforward.
