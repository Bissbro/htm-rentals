Apartments for Rent in Hulhumale' Phase 2 — Complete Guide

HTM Rentals · September 2026 · 5 min read

Hulhumale' Phase 2 has quickly become one of the most sought-after locations for short-term apartment rentals in the Maldives. Whether you're visiting for business, a family holiday, or an extended stay, finding the right apartment in this modern urban island offers comfort, convenience, and value that traditional resort hotels simply can't match.

Why Choose Hulhumale' Phase 2?

Hulhumale' Phase 2 is a planned urban development just minutes from Velana International Airport. Unlike the crowded Male' city centre, Phase 2 offers wide roads, modern buildings, and a peaceful atmosphere while still being connected to everything you need.

  • Easy airport access — just 10-15 minutes from Velana International Airport
  • Modern infrastructure — wide roads, reliable electricity and fast internet
  • Affordable compared to resorts — fully furnished apartments at a fraction of resort prices
  • Local experience — cafes, restaurants, supermarkets, and beach all within walking distance

Types of Apartments Available

Short-term rentals in Hulhumale' Phase 2 typically come in studio, one-bedroom, and two-bedroom configurations. Most quality apartments include air conditioning, high-speed WiFi, a fully equipped kitchen, washing machine, and Smart TV.

What to Look for When Renting

  • Location within Phase 2 — proximity to the beach, supermarkets, and main road
  • Fully private unit — avoid shared accommodation if you want peace and privacy
  • Direct booking option — booking directly with the host saves you OTA fees
  • 24/7 support — essential for international guests unfamiliar with the area
  • Verified host — check reviews on Airbnb, Booking.com or Google

Average Rental Prices in Hulhumale' Phase 2

Short-term apartment rentals in Hulhumale' Phase 2 typically range from MVR 800 to MVR 1,500 per night for a one-bedroom unit, depending on the building, amenities, and season. Two-bedroom units can range from MVR 1,500 to MVR 3,000 per night.

Booking directly with property managers like HTM Rentals often gives you better rates than OTA platforms, along with more flexibility on check-in times and special requests.

How to Book an Apartment in Hulhumale'

The easiest way to find a quality short-term apartment in Hulhumale' Phase 2 is to book directly through the property's website or contact them on WhatsApp. Platforms like Airbnb, Booking.com, and Agoda also list many properties, but direct booking saves commission fees and allows for more personalised service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book a short-term apartment in Hulhumale' for just one night?
Yes, most properties including HTM Rentals accept one-night bookings, though minimum stay requirements may apply during peak season.

Is Hulhumale' safe for tourists?
Hulhumale' is one of the safest places in the Maldives. It's a planned residential city with 24/7 security in most buildings and a low crime rate.

How do I get from the airport to Hulhumale' Phase 2?
The Sinamale' Bridge connects Hulhumale' to Male' and the airport island. Taxis are available at the airport and the journey takes around 15-20 minutes.
